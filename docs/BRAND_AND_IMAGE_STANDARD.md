# OUYANG SURGE Brand and Engineering-Image Standard

This repository uses the approved OUYANG SURGE brand pack as its formal visual reference.

## Brand assets

- Official logo source: `assets/brand/ouyang-surge-logo-master.png`.
- Keep the logo's proportions, colors, wording, and clear space unchanged. Do not redraw it, recolor it, stretch it, rotate it, or add shadows or glow effects.
- The visible brand wording is **OUYANG SURGE** with the descriptor **SURGE PROTECTION**.
- Core colors: deep blue `#003A8C`, bright blue `#00A3FF`, neutral gray `#666666`, and white.
- The site uses a clean industrial, mobile-first treatment with short technical tables and diagrams rather than decorative effects.

## Engineering-image governance

- Publish only original, licensed, or otherwise verified reusable images.
- Brand explanatory illustrations may show typical locations only. They must not assert an exact topology, MOV/TMOV rating, certification, customer, product ownership, or circuit position unless a source verifies it.
- Where an application diagram is used, include this qualification: “Typical protection location. Actual topology and component selection depend on system voltage, surge level, standards and protection coordination.”
- Third-party teardown screenshots and factory/company reference images in the source brand pack are research references only. They are not public-site assets and must not be published as OUYANG SURGE photography or evidence.
- Do not infer technical specifications, approvals, customer relationships, or manufacturing claims from an image.

## Commercial use

Primary calls to action remain: Discuss a MOV Application, Request Sample, WhatsApp, then Email. Site content should support the path: qualified inquiry → sample → qualification → RFQ → order.
